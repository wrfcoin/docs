# Open Questions Rollup

## Protocol and economics

- What reviewed policy implements Sybil/collusion resistance and slashing?
- How will geographic reward fairness be measured and enforced?
- Which, if any, L2 and data-sidechain designs will be selected?
- What versioned specification and activation gate would define WRC-20?

## Release and operations

- When do the generated endpoint/status artifacts match a deployed public cut?
- Which evidence closes faucet, snapshot-trust, operator-guide, secret-scan,
  visibility, and join-path gates?
- What measurable forecast-quality target closes the weather-oracle phase?

## Governance

- Who approves consensus parameter, issuance, slashing, and visibility changes?
- What evidence gates a future mainnet or production-service decision?

Until answered and implemented, these remain planned or partial—not current
testnet guarantees.
